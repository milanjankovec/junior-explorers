# Zdroje vzdelávacieho obsahu

Overené 16. novembra 2026.

- **Záchranári planéty, Bratislava.** Pravidlá označené mestom, vrátane triedených surovín a zálohovaných obalov. [Mesto Bratislava](https://bratislava.sk/zivotne-prostredie-a-vystavba/zivotne-prostredie/odpady/triedeny-zber), [OLO](https://www.olo.sk/sluzby/triedeny-zber-papiera-plastov-a-skla), [Správca zálohového systému](https://slovenskozalohuje.sk/).
- **Záchranári planéty, Praha.** Materiály do domácich pražských zberných nádob a miestne kombinácie. [Pražské služby](https://www.psas.cz/svoz-trideneho-odpadu), [FAQ Pražských služieb](https://www.psas.cz/faq-24).
- **Kto čo papá?** Príklady potravy vrátane jeleňa, vlka, grizlyho, zubra, bobra, pumy a vydry vychádzajú z materiálov National Park Service. [Yellowstone Eat Or Be Eaten](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/teachers/lessonplans/EatOrBeEatenSupplementLifeCards.pdf).
- **Kto čo papá?** Ďalšie príklady potravy overené v druhových záznamoch. [Slon ázijský](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/asian-elephant), [lev](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/lion), [vlk hrivnatý](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/maned-wolf).
- **Prírodný bádateľ.** Metamorfóza motýľov a žiab a rast plodín. Hra zjednodušuje fázy s ohľadom na vek a režim. [Smithsonian Gardens](https://gardens.si.edu/learn/for-educators/cultivating-habitat/butterfly-gardening/about-butterflies/), [American Museum of Natural History](https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/frogs-a-chorus-of-colors/reproduction/tadpoles), [Royal Horticultural Society](https://www.rhs.org.uk/get-involved/grow-with-it/pdfs/grow-with-it-support-pack.pdf).
- **Malý meteorológ.** Meranie a porovnanie teploty. Tabuľky a grafy používajú modelové pozorovania. [NOAA Weather Observations](https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/weather-atmosphere/weather-observations).
- **Malý obchodník.** Hodnoty euromincí, fiktívne ceny a schematické herné mince. [Európska centrálna banka](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/coins/html/index.en.html).

## Forest Tracks

The simplified track illustrations distinguish print shapes, toes, pads and hoof marks. The National Park Service, U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service and North Dakota Game and Fish publish examples of those features. [National Park Service animal tracks](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/animaltracks.htm) · [Sacramento National Wildlife Refuge field journal](https://www.fws.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2024-08/sacramento_nwr_wetland_walk_field_journal_all-ages_508.pdf) · [North Dakota wildlife tracks and signs](https://gf.nd.gov/sites/default/files/publications/tracks_signs_of_nd_wildlife.pdf)

- **Playground Shadows** uses a simplified model of a fixed post casting a shadow on level ground. The angle and position of the light change the size and direction of the shadow. [Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics elementary shadow activities](https://hea-www.harvard.edu/ECT/Shadow/shadow.html).
- **Magnet Workshop** uses a virtual magnet and explicitly identified materials. It illustrates differences between iron, ordinary magnetic steel and materials such as wood, plastic, copper or aluminum. [University of Kentucky science outreach activity](https://www.pa.uky.edu/sciworks/travelzoo/Magnets.htm).
- **Where Does Rain Go?** models downhill surface runoff on a small grid of fixed heights. [U.S. Geological Survey Water Science School](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/surface-runoff-and-water-cycle).

## Pitch, buoyancy, rotation and circuits

- **Musical Steps** uses synthesized pitches and a diagram that places higher notes on higher steps. [The Open University’s introduction to pitch](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/introduction-music-theory-2-pitch-and-notation/content-section-1).
- **Float or Sink?** models buoyancy for selected objects, including a solid ball and a hollow boat made from the same amount of modelling clay. [OpenStax on buoyancy and the clay boat example](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-4-archimedes-principle-and-buoyancy).
- **Day and Night** uses a simplified equatorial diagram to show how Earth's rotation takes a place into and out of sunlight. [NASA StarChild on day and night](https://starchild.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/StarChild/questions/question31.html).
- **Light a Bulb** models completed paths and open switches in virtual battery circuits with series or parallel lamps. [PhET Circuit Construction Kit: DC](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/circuit-construction-kit-dc).
